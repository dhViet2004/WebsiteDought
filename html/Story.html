DOUGH was born when five collegue friends, driven by a love for sweet treats and bold flavors, set out to reinvent the classic doughnut.

With nothing more than a shared dream and a tiny kitchen, they mixed, glazed, and tasted their way to the perfect bite. Their goal? To bring joy, creativity, and connection to every doughnut box.

From local roots to a growing community
What began as a weekend pop-up at university fairwell soon turned into a must-visit destination for doughnut lovers.

With the genius idea of structuring the bakery as an open-concept kitchen, Crumbl provides customers a first-hand experience watching as their cookies are mixed, balled, and baked! Eventually, Crumbl's business grew to include more flavors and services like delivery, catering, and a weekly rotating menu.

Our mission is simple: Spread Joy Through Every Doughnut
At DOUGH, we believe in crafting more than just desserts—we’re creating little moments of happiness.

Our menu rotates regularly, featuring handcrafted flavors inspired by seasonal ingredients, nostalgic treats, and bold, unexpected combinations. Every doughnut is made fresh with love, because we know that something as simple as a doughnut can turn someone’s day around.
